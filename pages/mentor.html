Our Mentor

Mike Cecot-Scherer

Senior Instructor, Creative Technology and Design Program

Mike Cecot-Scherer is a Senior Instructor in the Creative Technology and Design program at the University of Colorado Boulder's ATLAS Institute. With extensive experience in product design, engineering, and education, Mike brings a wealth of knowledge and practical expertise to his mentorship of student projects.

Throughout the development of the Nova Tent project, Mike has provided invaluable guidance in areas ranging from material selection and structural design to prototyping and testing. His expertise in sustainable design and manufacturing processes has been particularly instrumental in helping us create an environmentally conscious product that doesn't compromise on performance or durability.

"The Nova Tent project exemplifies the kind of innovative thinking and practical problem-solving that we strive to cultivate in our students. It's been incredibly rewarding to see Claire and William take on the challenge of creating a sustainable, user-friendly camping solution that addresses real-world needs."

Mike's teaching philosophy emphasizes hands-on learning and real-world application, which aligns perfectly with the project-based approach of the Nova Tent development. His mentorship has been crucial in helping us navigate the challenges of product development while maintaining our focus on sustainability and user experience.
