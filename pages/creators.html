Meet the Creators

Claire Grossman

Head of Radical Ideas & Sketch-to-Stitch Magician

Growing up camping, skiing, and exploring the outdoors, Claire understands how the right gear can transform an adventure. With a background in product and apparel design, along with a love for hands-on problem solving, she helped bring the Nova Tent from early sketches to the final stitch. Her attention to detail, creative mindset, and sewing experience all came together to refine the tent's structure and user experience. For Claire, designing the Nova Tent was about more than just functionality, it was about making outdoor adventures easier, more comfortable, and a lot more fun.



William Fitzgerald

Stargazing Seamster & Stoke Specialist

For Fitz, designing outdoor gear is as much about sparking adventure as it is about solving real-world challenges. With a background in creative technology, product design, and hands-on fabrication, he helped bring the Nova Tent from first prototypes to final production. As Nova Tent’s Stargazing Seamster and Stoke Specialist, Fitz combines technical sewing skills, a love of the outdoors, and a passion for creative problem-solving to craft gear that's durable, functional, and ready for anything. His goal with the Nova Tent was simple: to make camping more comfortable, spontaneous, and full of stoke under the open sky.
